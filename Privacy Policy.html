Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

SUMMERSET (“Summerset”, “we”, “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through the website, how it may be used, and how it may be protected.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy applies to information collected through the website, including that provided while browsing the website, contacting us, or placing an order.

Information we Collect

We may collect personal information that you provide when you contact us and place an order. This may include information such as:

We only collect information that is relevant to improving our website and responding to customer inquiries, suggestions, reports, and product orders.

How we Use your Information

The information we collect may be used for the following purposes:

We will not use personal information for purposes other than those described in this Privacy Policy.

Safety and Disclosure of your information

Summerset takes reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

We do not sell or share your personal information for unrelated purposes. Information may only be accessed or used when necessary to respond to inquiries, suggestions, reports, or product orders.

Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to inquiries, suggestions, reports, and processing orders.

When personal information is no longer necessary for these purposes, it may be deleted or disposed of through appropriate means.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

Summerset may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the website, its features, or its privacy practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page. We encourage visitors to review this Privacy Policy periodically for updates.
