About Summerset

Summerset is a Filipino T-shirt brand that brings the spirit of summer into everyday wear. Our sets of tees blend comfort with Filipino culture, using muted summer colors, beach-inspired elements and a minimalist look.

Our goal is simple: to give you an easy, accessible and enjoyable online shopping experience, from browsing the collection to placing your order.
